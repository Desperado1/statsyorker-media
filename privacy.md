# Privacy policy: Stats Yorker uploader

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The Stats Yorker uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Stats Yorker channels
(Instagram @statsyorker and the Stats Yorker YouTube channel) to manage those channels. It is not offered to anyone else.

**What it accesses.** The owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account: to upload the owner's videos, to read the
comments and statistics on those videos, and to post replies the owner has approved.

**Comments.** Comments on Stats Yorker videos are public. The tool reads them only to help the owner reply, keeps them
in the owner's private storage, and does not sell or share them or use them for advertising.

**Other people.** No data is collected from people who do not interact with Stats Yorker videos.

**Google API Services.** The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

**Contact.** Send a message to @statsyorker on Instagram.
