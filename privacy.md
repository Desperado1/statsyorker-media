# Privacy policy: Stats Yorker uploader

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The Stats Yorker uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Stats Yorker channels
(Instagram @statsyorker and the Stats Yorker YouTube channel) to manage those channels. It is not offered to anyone else.

## YouTube API Services

**This tool uses YouTube API Services.** By using it, you agree to be bound by the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).
Google's handling of data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**What it accesses on YouTube.** Only the owner's own channel: it uploads the owner's videos, reads the comments and
statistics (YouTube Analytics) on those videos, and posts comment replies the owner has approved.

**What it stores.** Video IDs, view and engagement statistics, and the text of public comments on the owner's videos,
kept in the owner's private storage to help the owner reply and track performance. Nothing is sold, shared with third
parties, or used for advertising. No data is collected from people who do not interact with Stats Yorker videos.

**Cookies and device data.** The tool runs on a server, has no website sign-in, and does not place cookies or read
information from your device.

**Revoking access.** The owner can revoke the tool's access to the YouTube account at any time from the Google
security settings page: <https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions>.

**Deleting data.** Stored data is deleted within 30 days of access being revoked, or sooner on request. Statistics
obtained from the API are refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, as the YouTube API Services Developer Policies
require. To ask for deletion of a comment you wrote that the tool stored, contact us (below).

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Instagram

On Instagram the tool accesses only the @statsyorker account (its posts, comments on them and their statistics) under
Meta's Platform Terms, with the same storage, sharing and deletion rules as above.
